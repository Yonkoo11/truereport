1
The problem

A Nigerian sick note is a letterhead, a stamp and a signature.

  • The Embassy of Canada's verification officer: reports lack security features and forgery is endemic (IRB Canada, 2025).
  • The Nigerian Senate asked the Health Ministry for a way to stop the proliferation of fake medical reports (2019).
  • Checking one today means phoning the hospital. A shared database would fix it by leaking health data.
2
How it works

Four steps. Only fingerprints reach the ledger.

1 · RegisterThe registrar adds hash(doctor key, name + MDCN folio) to a Merkle tree on Midnight.
2 · IssueThe doctor proves in zero knowledge their key is in the tree. Only a salted commitment and a revoke tag are written.
3 · PrintThe QR carries the printed lines and the salt. Never the diagnosis text.
4 · ScanThe browser rebuilds the commitment and reads the ledger through the indexer. No wallet, no server of ours.

Why Midnight

A public registry that cannot say which doctor wrote which report.

  • issueReport: ZK Merkle membership over a depth-10 tree; report hash, doctor id and salt stay private.
  • revokeReport: tag = hash(key, commitment), so only the issuer can revoke and reports stay unlinkable.
  • Dual ledger: keys, text and salts are private state; the tree, commitments and revocations are public.
  • Without it: a public chain names the doctor on every report, or the verifier trusts our database.
